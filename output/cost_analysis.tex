\newrevision{The AED 1,150.23 total sums the 24 items in Table~\ref{tab:bom}. It excludes AED 15 in Besomi shipping and a net AED 2.20 Amazon shipping and promotion adjustment. The ceramic capacitor values require confirmation before use in circuit calculations.}

\newrevision{The printed body, lid and piezo holder have reported masses of 150, 70 and 35 g, respectively, totaling 255 g (Table~\ref{tab:printed_enclosure_mass}). A material-only cost allocation is $0.255c$ AED if the confirmed filament price is $c$ AED/kg. The parts were printed in PLA/PLA+; the price per kilogram remains to be confirmed for this allocation. This allocation is not an additional purchase and does not change the reimbursement total; printing losses, machine time and electricity are separate costs.}
